Taxi Shift Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Taxi Shift (the "Game") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. The Game is published by Maham Khizar ("we", "us"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.

Your game progress (cash, cabs, upgrades, story progress and settings) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

2. Information collected automatically

To show ads, measure how the Game is used and fix problems, the Game and the services listed below may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Google Advertising ID on Android), the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and identifiers created by these services.
  • Device and network information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, country (derived from your IP address), IP address and mobile carrier.
  • Usage information: when the Game is opened, rides started, completed or failed, story days finished, items bought with in-game cash, and which ads were shown or watched.
  • Ad information: ad impressions, clicks and the estimated revenue of each ad.

3. How we use this information

  • To show ads, including ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards, and to limit how often ads appear.
  • To understand how players use the Game and improve its balance, difficulty and features.
  • To change game settings remotely (for example ad timing) without an app update.
  • To measure where installs come from and how well our advertising works.

We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Tracking and ad personalization choices

  • iOS: the Game asks for your permission before it uses your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Android: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Game.

6. Data retention

We keep usage data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Third-party services keep data according to their own retention policies (for example, Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months by default).

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, delete or correct information about you, to object to its use, or to opt out of its sharing for targeted advertising. Because we do not know who you are, please include your device's advertising ID or IDFV when you contact us, so the request can be matched. You may also contact the third-party services in section 4 directly.

9. Security

Data is sent to the services above over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to play the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
