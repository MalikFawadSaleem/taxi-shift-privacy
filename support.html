Taxi Shift Support

Help for the mobile game Taxi Shift, published by Maham Khizar.

Contact us

For bugs, questions or feedback, e-mail khizarmaham087@icloud.com. We usually reply within 2–3 working days.

Please include your device model, iOS version, the game version (shown in Settings) and a short description or screenshot of the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
Your cash, cabs, upgrades and story progress are saved only on your device. There are no accounts, so deleting the game also deletes your progress.

A rewarded ad did not give me my reward.
Rewards are only given after the ad has finished playing. Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, e-mail us with the time it happened.

How do I change the controls?
Open Settings from the main menu and choose steering wheel or arrow controls.

How do I stop personalised ads?
On iOS go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn tracking off for Taxi Shift. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

The game runs slowly or crashes.
Close other apps, restart your device and make sure you have the latest version from the App Store.
